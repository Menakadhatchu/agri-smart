🌿 Why Crop Rotation?

🌱 Legume Crops

Legumes help improve soil nitrogen and support soil health.

💧 Water-efficient Crops

Millets can be useful in areas with limited water availability.

🌍 Cover Crops

Cover crops help protect soil and reduce erosion.

🌾 Deep-rooted Crops

Some deep-rooted crops can access water from deeper soil layers.
